Prince Books Ads Manager

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Prince Books Ads Manager handles information when its owner uses it to manage advertising for their own books through the Amazon Ads API.

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who operates this application

Prince Books Ads Manager is operated by an independent author for that author's own book advertising. It is not a service offered to other advertisers.

Information used

When the owner authorizes access, the application may use Amazon Ads account and profile identifiers, campaign settings, advertised book identifiers, advertising reports, performance metrics, and authorization credentials needed to access the Amazon Ads API. It may also keep local records of actions and their outcomes to avoid duplicate requests and support troubleshooting.

How information is used

The information is used to view advertising accounts and reports, prepare and manage campaigns for the owner's books, and monitor campaign performance. Account actions are limited to the access granted by the account owner and the controls available through Amazon Ads.

Storage and access

The Beyondwords integration used by this application stores connection records in a private local workspace. API credentials are supplied through the owner's private environment or secret manager, rather than published on this website. The owner should restrict access to the computer and workspace where the integration runs.

Sharing

The application exchanges information with Amazon's advertising and authorization services to provide its functions. If the owner chooses to use an AI assistant to analyze advertising information, that information may also be processed by the assistant service under its own terms and privacy policy. The application does not sell Amazon Ads information.

Retention and removal

Local connection records and reports are kept while needed to operate and review the owner's advertising activity. The owner can stop use of the application, revoke its Amazon authorization, and delete local records. Revoking authorization stops future API access but does not automatically delete records already held locally.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's actual data handling changes. The updated date above will identify the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact princetewatia.cs@gmail.com.
